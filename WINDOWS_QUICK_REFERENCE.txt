SIMPLEST WAY (Windows Only):
  1. Connect Android device via USB
  2. Enable USB Debugging (Settings → Developer Options)
  3. Double-click: BUILD_AND_INSTALL.bat
  4. Wait 5-10 minutes
  5. App appears on your device!

ALTERNATIVE (Command Line):
  1. Open PowerShell
  2. cd d:\NishaanTech\state-quest-adventure\android
  3. .\gradlew.bat clean assembleDebug installDebug
  4. Wait 5-10 minutes
  5. Done!

VERIFY DEVICE CONNECTION:
  adb devices
  (Should show your device as "device", not "offline" or blank)

TROUBLESHOOTING:
  Device not detected?  → Enable USB Debugging, try different USB port
  Build timeout?        → Check internet connection, try again
  App won't launch?     → Check device logs: adb logcat
  No haptics?           → Check device vibration setting is ON
